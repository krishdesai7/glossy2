# Corpus

## ohthere/

*The Voyages of Ohthere and Wulfstan*, translated and glossed by Tyler Lemon.
75 sentences, 1,716 tokens. Copied byte-for-byte from commit `8659288` (2026-09-30)
of the original Glossy repo, the first commit containing these files.

- `Voyages_of_Ohthere_Wulfstan.tex`: gb4e source. Contains Old English text,
  morpheme segmentation, Leipzig glosses, translations and footnotes.
  It has no lemmas, parts of speech or Wiktionary links.
- `Voyages_of_Ohthere_Wulfstan.pdf`: compiled from the source on 2026-09-30.
  1,715 of 1,716 gloss tokens match the `.tex` in order. The exception is
  `have--prs.ind.pl`, a double hyphen in the source.
- `.bib`, `unified.bbx`, `unified.cbx`: needed to compile the `.tex`.

This is the original version on purpose. The later copy in the Glossy repo was
changed by commits under agent identities: it drops the "Use of different
resources" section, corrupts `$\sim$` to `$sim$`, and changes some glosses.

## Deliberately excluded

From `content/texts/` and `references/` in the Glossy repo:

- Beowulf prologue: not glossed by Tyler Lemon, despite the credit on the site.
  It comes from an agent-committed table in `scripts/compile-beowulf.mjs`.
- Cædmon's Hymn, The Wanderer: generated by `scripts/compile-presets.mjs` from the
  rule-based lemmatizer. Glosses are almost all just copies of the lemma.
- `ohthere.json`: its glosses derive from the `.tex`, but its lemmas, parts of
  speech, features and links came from the lemmatizer, with limited later edits.
  Do not treat it as gold data.
